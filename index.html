Nugget Tycoon Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Nugget Tycoon is a mobile game published by Nugget Labs Studio ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. In short: the game has no accounts, your progress stays on your device, and we run no servers that receive your data. Google provides the ads and the payments, and it handles the data described below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved on your device only. We do not receive or store them. You can erase them at any time with Settings → Reset Save in the game, or by uninstalling the game. Your phone's own backup feature may copy some game settings to your Google account, and you control that in your device settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • device and app information, such as device model and operating system version
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • how you interact with ads

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where allowed, personalise ads. Where the law requires your consent, for example in the European Economic Area and the UK, the game asks for it before any ad is requested. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Read more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment and keeps your purchase history under its own privacy policy. We never see your card or payment details. The game only receives confirmation of what you bought, so it can deliver the item and restore one-time purchases.

What we do not do

We do not ask you to create an account, we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location through the game, we do not include an analytics service of our own, and we do not sell personal information.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and delete it when it is no longer needed. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Children

Nugget Tycoon is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has contacted us with personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold your game data, there is nothing for us to access or delete beyond any emails you sent us. For data handled by Google, use your Google account settings or the links above. You may have further rights under the law where you live, and you can contact us about them at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
